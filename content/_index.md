I started as a sound engineer and built my career in the music industry: touring, studios, interactive installations. My work fuses art and technology, using digital signal processing to make sonic experiences for the spaces I work in.

I hold degrees in Cultures and Human Rights and in Electronic Music Composition, and in 2023 I completed the Master in Sound and Music Computing at the Music Technology Group in Barcelona, working on machine learning for sound and music.
