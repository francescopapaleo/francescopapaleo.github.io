Every year [CHEAP](https://www.cheapfestival.it/), the street poster art project from Bologna, launches a call for posters on a theme, and the best ones end up pasted on the city's walls. The 2026 theme was *conflitto e cura*: conflict and care.

[Undervilla](https://www.undervilla.com/) made the video presenting the selected posters, and I made the music for it.

A heavy theme for heavy times, and yet the posters that came out of it are anything but defeated. While the planet burns and warmongers sit in power, radical imagination keeps producing visions of possible futures. Being the soundtrack to that, even for a couple of minutes, felt like exactly the right place to be.
