Since 2022 I work as **Application Engineer** at [Holophonix](https://holophonix.xyz/) — an immersive audio processor developed by [L-Acoustics](https://www.l-acoustics.com/), designed for real-time spatial audio rendering in live performance, installations, and architectural contexts.

Holophonix supports a wide range of spatialization techniques — including Ambisonics (up to 7th order), Wave Field Synthesis, and binaural rendering — and integrates with OSC, MIDI, and show control systems.

My role spans pre-sales support, on-site commissioning, training, and technical consultation for venues and productions deploying the system.
