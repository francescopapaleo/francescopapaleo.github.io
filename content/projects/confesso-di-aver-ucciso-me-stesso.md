#### _studio per un trio attraversando me_

> Mi piacerebbe poter danzare senza soffrire. Confesso di aver ucciso me stesso e da qui inizierò a ritornare in me l'idea di bellezza del corpo, malgrado l'imperfezione, malgrado la repulsione, malgrado tutto.
>
> — Antonio Montanile

**Confesso di aver ucciso me stesso — studio per un trio attraversando me** is a choreography by [Antonio Montanile](https://www.stratagemmi.it/quattro-domande-ad-antonio-montanile/), presented as a finalist at the **Premio Equilibrio Roma 2010** (third edition) — Teatro Studio, Auditorium Parco della Musica, Rome, 6 February 2010.

I created and performed the **electroacoustic music live**: microphones placed on stage and on Antonio's body captured the sounds of the performance, which I processed in real time with effects and manipulations of every sort in Ableton Live. Nothing was pre-recorded — everything happened on stage, as it unfolded.
