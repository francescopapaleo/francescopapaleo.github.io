Dear friends,

This time I'd like to share a story.

Houghton Festival, summer 2024, somewhere in England.

John Talabot is at the controls. I've spent amazing days with people I love, with people who love the music as much as I do. At least, that's what we all feel.

It's the last set of the festival. Everyone's gathering around a tree and a small cabane, dancing until feet burn, until there's nothing left to dance to.

Talabot is playing and he knows how to drive us crazy.

Then it hits. That moment when a track locks into the groove so perfectly you forget you have a body.

## Hanss

And then it's packing, a bus, the good byes and see you soon! But you packed an obsession with you and it won't go away. Turns out all the above wasn't only a by-product, my friend Uri sends an audio a few days later.

Download isn't even finished before the file is chopped, sampled, warped, stretched. I had to remix it.

[![Mantix — Hanss (Francesco Papaleo Remix) cover artwork](/assets/img/mantix-hanss-remix.webp)](https://francescopapaleo.bandcamp.com/track/mantix-hanss-francesco-papaleo-remix-2)

Second release. A reworked, stretched and bent remix of Mantix's original. It won't be available to stream on any platforms apart from SoundCloud and [Bandcamp](https://francescopapaleo.bandcamp.com/track/mantix-hanss-francesco-papaleo-remix-2). Free download, pay what you feel.

**Why This Track?**

Mantix's original had this hypnotic quality I couldn't shake. Despite my best efforts to contact the artist, I never got a response—but the track kept calling me back. This remix is my tribute to a piece of music that clearly needed to exist in another form. I wanted to push it further: more tension, more space, more of that 3 AM clarity when the dancefloor becomes a collective organism.

If Mantix ever reads this: thank you for the inspiration. The track deserved this treatment.

### Collective intelligence and collaboration

The response to "Ain't No Sunshine" went beyond all my expectations. Everyone who shared it, commented, bought it, or just wrote back—you reminded me why I do this. Turns out screaming into the void is way more fun when the void occasionally screams back!

This remix went through 7 versions before I could let it go. The same constraints applied: self-imposed deadlines, trusted ears, and the occasional "just fucking finish it" from friends.

Special thanks to [**Timm Völkner**](https://timmvoelkner.com/) for the artwork once again—somehow still patient with my indecisiveness.

Shoutout to the usual suspects (you know who you are) for feedback, late-night voice notes, and reality checks.

Mastering by [**DAVE N.A.**](https://linktr.ee/daven.a), who continues to make my mess sound intentional.
